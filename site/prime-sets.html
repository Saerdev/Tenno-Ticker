How to read the prices

Median closed price is the median platinum amount in Relics.run's order_type: closed data for each set. Volume is the number of closed orders reported for that source day. 7-day change compares the unrounded closed-order median with exactly seven calendar days earlier, expressed as a percentage. Missing figures show —; open buy and sell orders are not included.

About the source and vault status

Relics.run publishes dated market-history files. The date above the table refers to the source day, not the time you opened this page. Prices are indicative, not guaranteed executable trades. Vaulted: Yes means the Warframe Wiki reports no relics for that set in normal drop tables. Special sources, owned relics and trading may still be available. Vault status is checked separately from prices; unavailable status shows —.
