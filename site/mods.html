How to read prices

Median closed price and Volume use Relics.run's reported order_type: closed records for the selected rank. Prices are rounded down to whole Platinum. 7-day change compares unrounded medians to exactly seven days earlier. No price or trade record is shown as —.

Acquisition notes

Acquisition locations and chances are drawn from WFCD's mod catalog. Syndicate filters match documented standing-vendor sources; missing or outdated vendor entries may affect results. Compare Rank 0 prices for unranked standing purchases, and check the Wiki for current availability and standing costs. Listings are market items, not Rivens, relics, or weapon parts.
