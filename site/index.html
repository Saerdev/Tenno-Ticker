How to read the board

Median closed price is the reported daily median for the selected rank, rounded down to whole Platinum. Volume is the reported daily closed-order count. 7-day change compares unrounded daily medians exactly seven days apart. Missing values display —. These statistics are not verified player-to-player trades.

⌁
About freshness

Arcane pricing is sourced from Relics.run daily history. The hourly Warframe.market scan still supplies arcane details. The refresh button only checks for newer published data. Arcane effects and acquisition sources come from WFCD and may omit vendors or time-limited events.
